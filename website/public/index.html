Who we serve

We specialise in small and mid-sized practices where the data is sensitive, the schedule is full and there is no in-house IT department.

Medical practices

Family medicine, pediatrics and specialty clinics that depend on their records system, imaging and a reliable front desk every day.

Dental practices

Digital imaging, practice-management servers and chairside workstations that must be available when the schedule starts.

Small professional offices

Any small business that wants the protection and support of a full IT team without hiring one.

What we do

Everything a practice needs from IT, handled for a predictable monthly fee.

24/7 monitoring

We watch performance, disk space, services and security events on every computer and server, and act on problems before your staff notice them.

Backup & recovery

Encrypted backups of practice servers and workstations, checked automatically, with files restorable on request when something goes wrong.

Encryption & data protection

Drive encryption on every computer, with recovery keys stored securely, so a lost or stolen device does not become a data breach.

Patching & security

Windows and software updates applied on a schedule, sign-in activity watched for anything unusual, and an audit trail you can show when asked.

Remote support

Secure, on-demand remote access means most problems are fixed in minutes, without a technician needing to drive to your office.

Servers & migrations

Planning, setting up and moving to new practice servers, with data carried over carefully and your vendors coordinated for you.
